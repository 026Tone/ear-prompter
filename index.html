Before you film
On your iPhone go to Settings → Camera → Record Sound and turn on Allow Audio Playback. Without it, the Camera app pauses this audio the moment you hit record.
Keep this page open in Safari, start playing, then switch to the Camera. The audio keeps going in your earbud.
